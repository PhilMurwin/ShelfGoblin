Shelf Goblin Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Shelf Goblin helps you catalog and track your physical book collection. This policy explains what information the Shelf Goblin web application handles when you use Google sign-in.

Information we handle

When you sign in, Google provides Shelf Goblin with the identity details shown by Google, such as your name, email address, Google account identifier, and optional profile image. Shelf Goblin uses these details only to identify the signed-in user in the application.

When a future Shelf Goblin feature needs to use your Shelf Goblin spreadsheet, Google may provide a temporary access token for the Google Sheets API. The application keeps that token only in browser memory and does not store it in localStorage or send it to a Shelf Goblin server.

Your Google data

Shelf Goblin requests only the Google Sheets authorization needed for its spreadsheet features. Shelf Goblin does not request access to Gmail, Calendar, Google Drive, or unrelated Google services. Your collection data is stored in your Google account and remains under your control.

How information is used and shared

We use Google identity information to display your signed-in state and use Google Sheets authorization to provide requested spreadsheet functionality. Shelf Goblin does not sell your information and does not share it with advertisers. The current application has no Shelf Goblin account database, analytics service, or custom authentication backend.

Retention and choices

Signing out clears the identity and temporary access token held by the current browser session. You can also remove Shelf Goblin’s Google authorization from your Google Account security settings. Google may retain information according to Google’s own policies.

Security

Shelf Goblin avoids persistent browser storage for OAuth access tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please use a current browser and protect your Google account credentials.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Shelf Goblin’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Shelf Goblin project through Shelf Goblin support.
